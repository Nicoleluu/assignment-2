BEAUTY / USE

If something is beautiful but difficult to use, does it still count as good design?

Some believe beauty creates meaning. Others believe usefulness is everything.

INTUITION / TESTING

Should designers trust their instincts?

Intuition shapes ideas. Testing challenges them.

FRICTION / EASE

Should good design always feel effortless?

Some experiences become meaningful because they require effort.

RESEARCH / BIAS

Can research ever be neutral?

Good designers don’t eliminate bias. They learn to recognize it.

POWER / ACCESS

Who gets considered in the design process?

Good design for one person may exclude another.
