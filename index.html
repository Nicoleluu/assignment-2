what counts as GOOD design?

Design is never one thing. It is function, feeling, access, bias, labor, beauty, testing, intuition, care.

BEAUTY / USE

If something is beautiful but difficult to use, does it still count as good design?

Some believe beauty creates meaning. Others believe usefulness is everything.

INTUITION / TESTING

Should designers trust their instincts?

Intuition shapes ideas. Testing challenges them.

FRICTION / EASE

Should good design always feel effortless?

Some experiences become meaningful because they require patience, slowness, and effort.

RESEARCH / BIAS

Can research ever be neutral?

Every method carries assumptions. Good designers don’t eliminate bias. They learn to recognize it.
